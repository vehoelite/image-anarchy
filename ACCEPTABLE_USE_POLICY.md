# Image Anarchy - Acceptable Use Policy

## 🏴 Our Philosophy: True Device Ownership

**You bought it. You own it.**

When you purchase a smartphone, tablet, or any electronic device, you exchange your hard-earned money for ownership of that device. Not a rental. Not a license. **Ownership.**

Yet manufacturers and carriers have increasingly locked down devices, preventing owners from:
- Installing the software they choose
- Modifying their own hardware
- Accessing their own data
- Repairing their own property

**We reject this.** Image Anarchy exists to restore the fundamental right of ownership to device owners.

---

## 📜 What Image Anarchy IS

Image Anarchy is a **digital rights tool** designed for:

- ✅ **Device Recovery** - Restore bricked devices, recover from failed updates
- ✅ **Custom Software** - Install custom ROMs, recoveries, and modifications on YOUR device
- ✅ **Repair & Restoration** - Restore factory functionality
- ✅ **Security Research** - Study and understand the devices you own
- ✅ **Data Recovery** - Access your own data on locked devices you own
- ✅ **Learning & Education** - Understand how your device works
- ✅ **Right to Repair** - Fix your own device without manufacturer approval

---

## 🚫 What Image Anarchy is NOT

Image Anarchy is **NOT** designed for and we **STRONGLY CONDEMN** use for:

- ❌ **Stolen Device Manipulation** - Changing IMEI to resell stolen phones
- ❌ **Fraud** - Evading blacklists, bypassing carrier restrictions on devices you don't own
- ❌ **Identity Theft** - Cloning device identities for malicious purposes
- ❌ **Circumventing Legal Obligations** - Evading court orders, law enforcement, or legitimate legal processes
- ❌ **Warranty Fraud** - Manipulating devices to falsely claim warranty coverage
- ❌ **Any Illegal Activity** - If it's illegal anywhere, it isn't welcome here (see Hard Lines)

## ⛔ Hard Lines

Some things Image Anarchy will not do, for anyone, anywhere. We hold one
worldwide standard: if something is illegal in *any* jurisdiction we know of,
we treat it as off-limits, even where your local law allows it.

### Factory Reset Protection (FRP) is never bypassed
FRP is the reason most lost phones get turned in instead of kept. A phone
locked to its owner's Google account is worthless to whoever finds or steals
it. We won't be the tool that changes that.

- Image Anarchy has **no** FRP erase or bypass feature.
- Every toolkit (ADB, Fastboot, MTK, Qualcomm EDL) **refuses** to erase or
  overwrite FRP partitions, including through custom/raw commands.
- Locked out of your own phone? Sign in with its Google account, or use Google
  account recovery. With proof of purchase, Google or the manufacturer can help.

### IMEI modification is not supported
Changing a device's IMEI is a crime in many countries (the UK, Australia,
Canada and others), even when "restoring" it. Image Anarchy has **no** IMEI
write feature. Reading the IMEI remains available so you can verify your own
device.

### Screen locks are never bypassed
Image Anarchy does not bypass PIN, pattern, password or biometric locks.

### Enforcement
- These limits are enforced in the software itself, not just in this policy.
- In the Image Anarchy community and remote-repair services, any attempt to
  bypass FRP or a screen lock, or to modify an IMEI, is an **automatic,
  permanent ban**.

### What's still supported
- **Network unlock with a carrier-issued code (NCK)** - entering the code your
  carrier gave you is the legal, intended way to unlock a phone you own.
